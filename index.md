healthcare workflows. Each new card includes safeguards, detection, evidence limits, and a
question for community partnership.

- **Recent incidents:** Boston Scientific, iRhythm, and Stryker; plus later Synnovis findings.

## Know what the evidence says

**Incidents** describe reported events. **Vulnerabilities** describe software weaknesses.
**Research** reports experimental findings. **Scenarios** help teams test plausible failures.
These labels keep potential harms distinct from documented outcomes.
