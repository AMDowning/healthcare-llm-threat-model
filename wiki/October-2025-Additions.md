# October 2025 research additions

The eight historical research cards are maintained in
[the October 2025 collection](../additions/2025-10/index.md).
Use those cards for citations, mitigations, and evidence limitations.

These entries describe research findings, including preprints. They are not
confirmed healthcare incidents or evidence that a vulnerability affected a
deployed system. A CVE assignment is a separate evidence category, not a
required next stage for a research finding.

Browse the [complete catalog](../threats/index.html) for current incidents,
software vulnerabilities, research, and illustrative scenarios. Follow the
[contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) to suggest corrections or updates.
